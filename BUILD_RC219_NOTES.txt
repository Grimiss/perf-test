D8M4 RC219 — LONG-FORM EVOLUTION PASS

AUTO MIX
- Original preset is no longer a hard ceiling or constant recovery target.
- Slow phase-based evolution across channel volume/pan and Character.
- Musical phase types include sparse, intense, redistributed, featured-track,
  ebb and free-drift states.
- Compensation is probabilistic: a drop may cause another track to rise, but
  sometimes nothing compensates and sometimes multiple tracks recede together.
- Values can travel substantially above or below their original preset levels.
- Movement is deliberately slow with smooth start/end curves.
- Roughly every 15–20 minutes the mix may drift near the original preset, but
  never performs a hard reset and then continues evolving.
- Sparse phases can gently expose more AGE/HISS.

AUTO FX
- Independent from AUTO MIX.
- No longer always drops selected effects to zero then restores the old level.
- Each event moves the selected 1/2/3/ALL/RND effects toward a new evolving
  destination and leaves the new state in place.
- Includes tremolo-focus, delay/reverb wash, redistribution, ebb, surge and
  free-evolution states.
- Some events push processing up, some down, some exchange energy between
  effects, and some make no compensating rise.
- Rare near-zero and near-full destinations remain possible.
- Each selected effect has its own slow move/hold timing so changes do not occur
  in lockstep.
- Recent effects remain less likely to be selected again immediately.
- AUTO FX continues to temporarily own an effect so SpE leaves it alone, then
  releases it to SpE after the new level has been reached and held.
- Soft near-home visits can occur around 15–20 minute intervals without an exact reset.

No UI/layout changes in this release.
